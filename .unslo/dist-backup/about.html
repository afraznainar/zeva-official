Where rice meets spice

Flavours of the Gulf, on Morgans Gate Road

Zeva Mandi & Grills is a Turkish and Arabic themed multi-cuisine restaurant in Bolar, Mangaluru, bringing the flavours of the Gulf to the heart of the city — tender meat slow-cooked and smoked over fragrant basmati rice with Arabian spices.

Mandi is the heart of the menu, but the kitchen goes further: charcoal grills and tandoor kebabs, Indian gravies, Indo-Chinese fried rice and noodles, pasta, fresh juices and mojitos. Guests talk about the friendly service and the ambience — 4.7 stars from 82 Google reviewers and counting.
